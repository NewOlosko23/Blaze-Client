# Blaze Briquettes - Complete Website Documentation

## 🔥 **About Blaze Briquettes**

### **What is Blaze Briquettes?**
Blaze Briquettes is a premium charcoal briquettes business based in Homabay, Nyanza, Kenya. We specialize in manufacturing and distributing high-quality, eco-friendly charcoal briquettes made from 100% recycled materials. Our products are designed for cooking, heating, and grilling applications across Kenya.

### **Our Mission**
To provide sustainable, high-quality fuel solutions that reduce environmental impact while meeting the cooking and heating needs of Kenyan households and businesses.

### **Our Vision**
To become Kenya's leading provider of eco-friendly charcoal briquettes, contributing to environmental sustainability while supporting local communities.

## 🎯 **What We Do**

### **Core Business**
Blaze Briquettes manufactures and sells premium charcoal briquettes made from recycled agricultural waste and other sustainable materials. Our products are designed for:

#### **Primary Uses:**
- **Cooking**: Perfect for home cooking, restaurants, and food service
- **Heating**: Reliable heat source for homes and businesses
- **Grilling**: Excellent for BBQ and outdoor cooking
- **Industrial**: Suitable for large-scale heating applications

#### **Product Benefits:**
- **Long Burning Time**: 4-6 hours of consistent heat
- **High Heat Output**: Reaches 450°C peak temperature
- **Minimal Ash**: 90% less ash than traditional charcoal
- **Easy to Light**: Quick ignition and consistent burning
- **Eco-Friendly**: Made from 100% recycled materials
