#### **Tracking Capabilities:**
- ✅ **Page Views** (URL, title, timestamp, referrer)

- ✅ **Time on Page** (Session duration tracking)

### **Cookie Categories Implemented**

#### **Essential Cookies (Always Active)**
- **Purpose**: Website functionality
- **Examples**: Session management, form data, security tokens
- **Cannot be disabled**: Required for basic functionality

#### **Analytics Cookies (Optional)**
- **Purpose**: Understanding user behavior
- **Data Collected**: Page views, time spent, click patterns, device info
- **Tools**: Google Analytics, custom tracking, heat mapping
- **User Control**: Can be disabled in preferences

#### **Marketing Cookies (Optional)**
- **Purpose**: Personalized advertisements
- **Data Collected**: Campaign effectiveness, retargeting, social media
- **Platforms**: Facebook Pixel, Google Ads, social media
- **User Control**: Can be disabled in preferences
